## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Fiber optic splicing and termination works are progressing well on the OYA Wind Energy Facility. As of 10 June 2026, splicing has been completed at WTG-07, WTG-08, and WTG-09, with 76 splices and 34 AC→LC connector changes completed on the reporting day alone.

OTDR testing confirms all inter-turbine spans from WTG-01 through WTG-04 are testing within specification. One fiber fault has been identified on the WTG-01 to PV route and is scheduled for remedial action before final handover. All remaining turbines (WTG-04 through WTG-18) are scheduled for completion by the 19 June 2026 deadline.

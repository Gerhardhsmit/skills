## How the score works

Each entity is scored 1–5 on six dimensions and weighted:

| Dimension | Weight | What a 5 means |
|---|---|---|
| **S** Spread & remoteness | 20% | Many sites / large area, far from town, terrain-challenged |
| **V** Operational value at risk | 25% | Time-critical, high-value operations where blindness costs money (cold chain, pumps, harvest, stock) |
| **G** Private-backbone fit | 15% | Value lives where current connectivity can't reach; a client-owned backbone clearly carries operations |
| **P** Ability to pay / scale | 15% | Group-scale business that can fund infrastructure capex |
| **D** Decision-maker access | 10% | Named senior decision-maker + published or strongly patterned email |
| **T** Timing trigger | 15% | Live 2025–26 event: new CEO, expansion, flood/loss, acquisition, systems hire |

**Probability band** = INTERPRETATION: estimated chance of converting to a paid assessment / network design within ~6–9 months, *if* the Outreach Quality Gate passes and outreach is executed well. It is a ranking aid, not a forecast.

| Weighted score | Band | Est. probability |
|---|---|---|
| ≥ 4.20 | **A** | 25–35% |
| 3.90–4.19 | **B** | 15–25% |
| 3.50–3.89 | **C** | 10–15% |
| 3.00–3.49 | **D** | 5–10% |
| < 3.00 | **E** | < 5% — park |

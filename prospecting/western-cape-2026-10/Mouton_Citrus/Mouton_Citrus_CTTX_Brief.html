The approach

Your own private backbone, on a carrier-grade link

A dedicated carrier-grade link brought to Brakfontein. From there, Mouton's own private network runs along the valley to the second packhouse, the CA and cold-store controls, the river pump stations and the farm offices. One backbone carries operations systems, packline data, alarms, cameras and voice. Mouton owns it, and CTTX supports it under SLA.

Cold chain you can see from anywhere

CA atmosphere, temperature and power alarms reach the right person immediately, from the packhouse or from off site, including when the valley is cut off.

Pumps and water across your farms

Flow, pressure and pump status from every station on one network, so a fault is found in minutes, not on the next drive past.

Traceability that keeps up

Field, packhouse and export office on one network, so the systems your operations team is building work at every site, not only at head office.

Owned, not rented

The network is your asset, built to match the carrier link and supported by CTTX under SLA. Where terrain allows, a second path out of the valley, and relay sites on solar to match your existing 550 kVA investment.

Proof that fits: Sandy Mountain, 33,000 ha

From a consumer service at 3/8 Mbps to 100 Mbps symmetrical carrier backhaul and an 800+ Mbps backbone across the property, with off-grid solar relays under battery telemetry. Call-outs went to zero.

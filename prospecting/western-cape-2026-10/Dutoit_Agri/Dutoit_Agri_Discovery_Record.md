## 2. How this business makes and loses money

**Value creation.**
- FACT: Grows, packs, stores and markets pome fruit (apples, pears), stone fruit, cherries ("Cherry Time" brand) and vegetables; export and local channels; Dutoit Europe/International sells abroad. Sources: dutoit.com extracts; FreshPlaza (above).
- FACT: Packhouses and cold stores earn on own fruit and on third-party fruit (Novo serves third-party exporters). Source: dutoit.com packing-facilities extract.
- INTERPRETATION: margin is made in three places — orchard yield and pack-out (class 1 export share), storage (CA lets apples/pears be sold over many months), and packhouse/cold-store throughput and utilisation (including third-party revenue at Novo).

**Time-critical windows.**
- FACT: Pome harvest/export season runs from about January; the 2026 season tracked 7–10 days early. Source: https://www.freshplaza.com/africa/article/9815038/hail-and-wind-reduce-top-fruit-estimates-in-langkloof-and-ceres/ (2026-10-02).
- INTERPRETATION: CA storage carries the fruit from harvest (Feb–Apr) into the following months; a failure in a sealed CA room is discovered late and lost in bulk.

**Loss and risk exposure (evidenced).**
- FACT: 2026 season — "both the Eastern and Western Cape experienced hail, severe heat and early size concerns"; a Week-6 hail and windstorm cut Ceres crop by an estimated 5–15% and Langkloof by 12–17%, with winds to 120 km/h damaging netting and farm infrastructure. Source: FreshPlaza 9815038 (above).
- FACT: 10–12 May 2026 storm — Ceres weather station recorded 333.2 mm on 11 May (record since 1955). Hortgro estimated R6.3 bn damage to the pome and stone fruit industry with 6,700 jobs at risk; Minister Steenhuisen toured with directors of Ceres Fruit Growers, **Dutoit Agri** and De Keur. Sources: https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-05-27-ceres-record-breaking-storm-fuels-agriculture-fears-in-western-cape/ ; https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/agri-news/south-africa/true-cost-of-may-floods-in-western-cape-still-unfolding/ (2026-10-02).
- FACT: The storm collapsed 6–10 pylons at Waaihoek (Wolseley–Worcester), cutting regional supply to 15–20% of normal, with repairs estimated up to four weeks. More than 300,000 t of export fruit sat in cold storage plus 100,000 t awaiting processing; the industry ran generators, burning about R2 m of diesel a day for about 50 days. Sources: https://www.freshfruitportal.com/news/2026/05/19/western-cape-flood/ ; https://iol.co.za/business-report/economy/2026-05-19-western-cape-storms-trigger-electricity-crisis-threatening-fruit-exports-and-jobs/ ; https://www.foodformzansi.co.za/infrastructure-crisis-deepens-strain-on-storm-battered-fruit-farmers/ (2026-10-02). These are industry/regional figures, not Dutoit's own.
- FACT: In the May floods, farmers lost "orchards, vineyards, roads, pumps and expensive netting"; higher-lying Op-die-Berg/Koue Bokkeveld farms escaped relatively unharmed, lower Ceres–Wolseley areas were hit hard. Source: Farmers Weekly (above).
- FACT: Sunburn from heat spikes is the Koue Bokkeveld's biggest challenge; "extremes in heat, rainfall and hail have become the new normal". Sources: FreshPlaza 9697989; Food For Mzansi (above).
- FACT: Dutoit ran the "Hail to Heroes" campaign with Good Hope Fruit and Pick n Pay to sell hail-marked apples. Source: https://www.dutoit.com/south-african-growers-and-retailer-create-campaign-around-hail-damaged-apples/ — INTERPRETATION: hail directly erodes export pack-out; the group has had to create a channel for downgraded fruit.
- UNKNOWN: Dutoit's own 2026 hail loss, its May 2026 generator hours/diesel spend, and any CA room incidents.

**Water and sustainability.**
- FACT: Dutoit has an innovation hub on soil health and water use and a water-retention strategy; Koue Bokkeveld is a WRAP/WWF Water Roadmap priority catchment supplying UK retailers (Worldwide Fruit). Source: https://www.worldwidefruit.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/250409-Koue-Bokkeveld-Case-Study-2-1-compressed-1.pdf (extract, 2026-10-02).

## 3. Niche economics (SA export pome fruit + CA storage)

- FACT: 2026 national estimates after the weather: apples passed for export ~49.4 m cartons (flat), pears 20.6 m (−11%). Source: FreshPlaza 9815038.
- FACT: SA CA regimes — e.g. rooms must reach 3% O2 + 1.5% CO2 within 4 days of sealing (N2 flushing); dynamic CA (DCA) runs fruit close to its low-oxygen limit for longer storage. Sources: Hortgro CA Operators' Manual https://postharvest.hortgro.co.za/pag/south-african-controlled-atmosphere-storage-operators-manual/ ; https://www.gasatsite.co.za/harvestwatch-dca-cf (2026-10-02).
- INTERPRETATION: the closer a store runs to the low-O2 limit, the more its value depends on continuous gas/temperature monitoring and fast alarms — and on those alarms reaching someone when the grid, generators or public networks are under stress.
- FACT: Weather extremes (hail, heat, May 2026 floods) and grid fragility are the live shocks for Ceres growers in 2026 (sources in section 2).
- FACT: Packhouse traceability runs on FarSoft ViTrax at Dutoit since 2015 (Dutoit Agri, then the Johannesburg depot, then Dutoit Groente in 2021) — bin tracking, RF scanning, pallet tracking, cold-store spot allocation. Source: https://farsoft.co.za/site/tag/vitrax/ (extract, 2026-10-02).

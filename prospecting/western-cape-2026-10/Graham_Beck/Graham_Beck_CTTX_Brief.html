The approach

We bring a dedicated carrier-grade link to the cellar and tasting-room cluster at Madeba. From there we build Graham Beck's own private network across the estate: one backbone for the cellars, irrigation, the reserve, solar and guests. It is owned by you and supported by us under SLA.

Stock you can see, any hour

Cellar temperature, humidity, power and access alarms, plus CCTV. The cellar team and management see them wherever they are.

Water you can prove

Pump, flow and soil-moisture readings from the 400 ha report live. The per-hectare budget and recycled volumes are on record, not assembled later.

A reserve with current evidence

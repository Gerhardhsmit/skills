## 2. How this business makes and loses money

**Value creation**
- Premium Cap Classique under the Graham Beck brand, sold locally and exported. Sources say exports go to "more than 50 countries" (wine.co.za 2026) or "40 countries, over 50% of production exported" (older search summary). The **USA** is the lead export market (BFE runs its own US import company), followed by the UK and Japan. FACT: https://news.wine.co.za/news.aspx?NEWSID=46578 ; https://wineindustryadvisor.com/2019/08/26/beck-family-launches-us-sales-import-company/ ; search summary of pleasethepalate.com
- **Cellar-door and tourism:** a tasting room that doubles as an art gallery, open 7 days (Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00, Sat–Sun 10:00–16:00). Paid tastings run R80–R180 pp, including popcorn and olive-oil pairings. There is also a membership club (society@grahambeck.com). FACT: https://tour.wine.co.za/taste/taste.aspx?TASTEID=603 ; grahambeck.com/estate/the-tasting-room (search summaries)
- **Group hospitality:** Steenberg (Constantia) has a five-star hotel, spa, two restaurants (Bistro Sixteen82, Tryn) and an 18-hole golf course, on 165 ha with about 65 ha of vines. FACT: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steenberg_Estate ; https://www.timeout.com/cape-town/hotels/steenberg-hotel-spa

**What protects margin (INTERPRETATION from the facts above)**
- Price premium depends on **brand equity and consistency**. The CEO's stated priority is "protecting the intrinsic value of our brands, safeguarding quality, reinforcing premium positioning". FACT: https://www.wineland.co.za/playing-the-long-game/ (4 May 2026); https://finance.yahoo.com/news/beck-family-estates-build-sustained-124256324.html
- **Working capital sits in the cellar.** Time on lees is about 15 months (Classic), 40 months (Vintage) and 60 months (Icon). FACT: grahambeck.com/estate/the-cellar (search summary). INTERPRETATION: at any moment Madeba holds up to five vintages of bottled, unreleased stock. That stock carries all the cost of grapes, labour and time but cannot yet be sold, so a temperature excursion, a water event or a theft hits value that took years to build.
- **Sustainability is part of the brand story in its export markets** (WWF Conservation Champion since 2004, "environmental leader"). FACT: grahambeck.com/legacy/initiatives/environmental ; grahambeckusa.com/sustainability (search summaries). The CEO's mandate includes "deepen sustainability commitments and enhance destination-led tourism and hospitality experiences". FACT: https://news.wine.co.za/news.aspx?NEWSID=46492

**Cost drivers**
- Energy: the 312 kWp solar PV on the Madeba 3 cellar roof covers **16%** of total consumption and saved over R305,000 in year one. FACT: https://www.solareff.co.za/projects/graham-beck-wine-estate/. INTERPRETATION: the remaining ~84% is grid (Eskom/Langeberg Municipality) or backup. Sparkling production needs cooling (fermentation, cold stabilisation, a cool cellar for lees ageing), so energy is a material cost line.
- Water: Robertson gets about 250–400 mm of rain a year, so the vineyards depend on Breede River irrigation. FACT (regional): https://www.winewithseth.com/winewiki/robertson/. Graham Beck recycles all farm and cellar sewage water (about 3 million L/month) for irrigation and caps use at about 6,000 m³/ha/year. FACT: grahambeck.com/legacy/initiatives/environmental (search summary)
- Labour, packaging (glass, cork, cage), export logistics: UNKNOWN specifics.

**Cycles and time-critical windows**
- Harvest for Cap Classique base wine is early (picked at lower sugar). The exact window is UNKNOWN; ASSUMPTION: January–February. Intake timing drives base-wine quality.
- Tourism peaks in summer, on weekends and in holidays (INTERPRETATION).

**Pipeline context:** in May 2026 Hanno Jordaan (IT Manager) asked CTTX for a tower upgrade survey.

- FACT: The family has farmed the upper Olifants River Valley for eight generations. Source: https://www.freshplaza.com/north-america/article/9611767/citrus-growers-association-of-southern-africa-welcomes-new-chairman-gerrit-van-der-merwe/
- FACT: Gerrit van der Merwe Snr (a founder and chairman of ALG) died at 68 after an accident on holiday in Malawi. He is credited with developing the US market for Western Cape citrus. The article is undated in the snippet; INTERPRETATION: about 2018, based on "44 years" since 1974. Source: http://www.fruitnet.com/eurofruit/article/177474/south-africans-in-shock-at-citrus-leaders-sudden-passing
- FACT: ALG has a marketing arm, "ALG Marketing". Source: http://www.algestates.com/alg-marketing/
- FACT (registry snippet): Citrus Select (Pty) Ltd, a packhouse at Farm Groenkloof, Citrusdal, lists directors Albertus Jacobus Wilhelmus van der Merwe and Gerrit Erasmus van der Merwe. ### 2. Location
- FACT: Upper reaches of the Olifants River Valley, Citrusdal. Source: https://www.algestates.com/
- FACT: Kardouw (Cartouw) is one of the ALG farms, alongside Karnemelksvlei and others. ALG has 4 main divisions. Source: https://winemag.co.za/wine/opinion/sa-wine-history-concluding-the-olifants-river-journey-for-now/ and https://www.algestates.com/alg-farms/
- FACT: Fruit comes from three production areas within a 75 km radius, each with its own microclimate. Citrusdal is 170 km from the Port of Cape Town. Source: https://freshplaza.com/article/172879/ALG-Estates-celebrates-its-250th-anniversary
- GPS: UNKNOWN. Not retrieved.

### 3. Size
- FACT (2010-era): 25 commercial varieties on 600 ha, latest yield 15,800 t, own juice/oil processing plant with capacity of at least 20,000 t a year. Source: https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/crops/field-crops/all-year-citrus-for-the-customer/
- FACT (circa 2018, 250th-anniversary article): about 18,000 t a year produced, packed and marketed; 41 varieties; citrus available every month of the year; 120 full-time staff and up to 800 seasonal workers at peak packing; 60 to 65% exported. Source: https://freshplaza.com/article/172879/ALG-Estates-celebrates-its-250th-anniversary
- FACT (2025): US tariffs could force removal of ALG orchards along the Olifants River and affect seasonal work for about 500 workers. Source: https://www.freightnews.co.za/article/citrusdal-faces-a-bleak-future-without-us-citrus-benefits (search summary)
- Current hectares: UNKNOWN. The 600 ha figure dates from 2010.

### 4. Business model
- FACT: Navels, Valencias, Midknights, grapefruit, lemons and soft citrus; harvest begins in March and runs about ten months. Source: https://www.algestates.com/
- FACT: First packhouse built in 1994, after ALG became the first Citrusdal group to break away from the co-op. Source: https://www.algestates.com/
- FACT: Supplies retailers including Woolworths. Source: https://www.algestates.com/
- FACT: A key supplier to the US "Summer Citrus from South Africa" programme; Gerrit Jnr is named as a grower there. Source: https://www.summercitrus.com/meet-our-growers/
- FACT: National Agricultural Writers' Farmer of the Year 2010. Source: https://www.gov.za/news/media-advisories/deputy-minister-guest-speaker-dinner-celebration-alg-estates-accolade-south
- Cold storage: UNKNOWN. INTERPRETATION: a packing and exporting operation of this size very likely has on-site cooling.

### 5. Spread and remoteness
- FACT: Three production areas spread over a 75 km radius (source above).
- INTERPRETATION: The upper Olifants valley is enclosed by the Cederberg mountains. A 75 km spread across separate microclimate zones means several sites without line of sight between them. That is a classic backbone-plus-relay design case.

### 6. Operational drivers and loss exposure
- FACT: US export exposure. The 30% US tariff took effect in August 2025. Gerrit Jnr estimated the volume impact at about a third of the previous year's US shipments. Source: https://www.freshplaza.com/north-america/article/9765839/south-african-citrus-exports-to-u-s-drop-on-tariffs/
- FACT: The US then exempted oranges from the tariff (November 2025), and Van der Merwe welcomed the relief. Source: https://iol.co.za/business-report/economy/2025-11-17-citrus-industry-welcomes-us-tariff-exemption-on-south-african-orange-exports/
- FACT: Floods in 2023 and 2024 cut Citrusdal off and disrupted the harvest and export flow. Source: https://www.news24.com/southafrica/news/western-cape-floods-citrus-farmers-worry-about-harvest-as-citrusdal-remains-cut-off-20240718
- FACT: Rural crime rises in harvest season as migrant workers almost double the district's population. Source: https://www.farmersweekly.co.za/rural-insight/citrusdals-tragic-harvest/
- INTERPRETATION: US-programme fruit needs strict cold-treatment and phytosanitary traceability; an orchard-to-packhouse data trail is a compliance asset. 800 seasonal workers means heavy demand for time-and-attendance and access control. A year-round, 41-variety pack schedule raises the cost of any network outage at the packhouse.

- FACT: The US 30% tariff ran from August 2025; oranges were exempted in November 2025.

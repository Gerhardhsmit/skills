## 2. How this business makes and loses money

**Value creation (FACT unless marked):**
- Grows, processes and exports bulk organic rooibos **direct to specialty and premium tea brands** in Europe, America and Asia — not via a co-operative or large processor. FACT — [why-buy-from-us](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/why-buy-from-us/), M3 advert.
- Sells single-origin estate consistency, a direct supply chain, and "carbon net-zero" rooibos. FACT — [carbon-net-zero-rooibos-supplier](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/carbon-net-zero-rooibos-supplier/).
- Product: Super Export Grade short/teabag cut and long/loose-leaf cut; also wild rooibos (supplied in larger volume from 2012 after a 2010 veld fire). FACT — [rooibos-processing-packing](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/rooibos-processing-packing/), [rooibos-regrowth-after-veld-fire](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/rooibos-regrowth-after-veld-fire/).

## 3. Niche economics

- Rooibos grows commercially only in the Cederberg/Sandveld region. Exports passed **10,000 t for the first time in 2025**, to 50+ markets; Japan is the largest overseas buyer; China, Indonesia and Sri Lanka growing. FACT — [Daily Maverick, 2026-02-26](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2026-02-26-rooibos-exports-soar-even-as-climate-stress-squeezes-cederberg-supply/).
- Production fell from **17,000 t (2023) to 15,000 t (2025)** under climate stress. FACT — same. Klipopmekaar republished this on its own site. FACT — [rooibos-industry](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/rooibos-industry/).
- Climate modelling: shorter winter water availability, and loss of 49.8%–88.7% of bio-climatically suitable rooibos area by 2070. FACT — summarised on their climate page, from [UCT/PMC research](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4020683/).
- INTERPRETATION: demand outruns supply, so a grower who can deliver reliable volume, organic and verified, is in a strong position; reliable irrigation is what protects that volume.
- Buyer/certification evidence: Rainforest Alliance requires all certified purchases and sales to be recorded in its online traceability platform (MultiTrace covers rooibos and herbal teas). FACT — [Rainforest Alliance MultiTrace guidance](https://knowledge.rainforest-alliance.org/docs/multitrace-traceability-user-manual-guidance), [herbs & spices program](https://www.rainforest-alliance.org/business/certification/herbs-and-spices-program/). FSSC 22000 requires documented, traceable process records (FACT per Klipopmekaar processing page). Organic (EU/NOP/JAS) requires lot traceability and annual inspection (INTERPRETATION from standard practice; confirm specifics).

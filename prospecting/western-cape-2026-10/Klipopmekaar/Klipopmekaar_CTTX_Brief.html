The approach

Your own private backbone, on a carrier-grade link

A dedicated carrier-grade link brought to your processing yard, then a network you own reaching the pump stations, dams, tea court and staff village on solar-powered relays, as the rest of the farm already runs. One backbone for irrigation telemetry, plant records, cameras, voice and staff Wi-Fi, supported by CTTX under SLA. Built in phases, starting where it pays first.

Irrigation you can see

Pump, solar, level and pressure readings with alarms, so a fault is found in hours, not on the next drive-out. This is the volume that keeps your buyers supplied in a dry year.

Records that keep up with the tea

Batch, pasteurising and dispatch records flow from the plant to your office and buyers on a link built for it, ready for audits and the Rainforest Alliance platform.

The farm from anywhere

Live view of the yard, tea court and plant in harvest, without the drive over Pakhuis Pass, plus the metering that backs your carbon claim each year.

Owned, not rented

The network is Klipopmekaar's asset, sized for a family business and built in stages, with CTTX support under SLA rather than a monthly service per building.

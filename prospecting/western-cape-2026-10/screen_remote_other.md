GPS: UNKNOWN.
3. **Size:** 6,000 ha of Cederberg mountain land (FACT: [about-us](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/about-us/)). It produces "hundreds of tons" of organic rooibos (FACT: same source). Staff numbers: UNKNOWN.
4. **Business model:** Farms, processes and exports organic rooibos direct to specialty and premium tea brands worldwide. Processing runs under FSSC 22000 (FACT: [klipopmekaar.co.za](https://www.klipopmekaar.co.za/rooibos-tea-processing/)). Mainly export.

Pattern: first@klipopmekaar.co.za.

Prepared for: CTTX Services (Gqeberha) | Date: 2026-10-02 | Tier: SCREEN (desk research only, no contact made)

**Method and limits.** Public web search only. No Apollo or paid enrichment was used. Direct page fetches were blocked by the network egress proxy for most domains (tad.co.za, dutoitagri.co.za, freshplaza.com, fruitnet.com). Most FACTs below therefore rest on search-engine extracts of the cited pages. Treat them as "source-indicated" and re-verify on the page before outreach. Labels: **FACT** (with source), **INTERPRETATION** (my reasoning), **UNKNOWN**. Email patterns are **INFERRED** from domains seen in data-broker listings, where the addresses are masked. No full addresses were published for named people.

**Segment rule applied.** Each entity is researched as itself. Olive economics (one harvest window around Mar–Jun, mill uptime and same-day pressing, dam and drip irrigation, hand or machine harvest) are kept separate from pome, stone and grape economics (CA cold storage, packhouse throughput, export cold chain, GLOBALG.A.P. traceability, hail and frost).

## Cross-segment notes (INTERPRETATION)
- **Fruit vs olive intensity.** Deciduous groups run year-round CA storage, so 24/7 monitoring loss exposure exists, and they operate multi-farm logistics. Olive estates concentrate risk in a 3–4 month harvest and milling window and in irrigation. Fruit groups are the stronger network buyers. Olives fit only at the 200 ha+ scale (Willow Creek, Mont Rouge) or where remoteness is extreme.
- **Ceres/Koue Bokkeveld cluster.** Dutoit, De Keur, Graaff and CFG's 38 shareholders form one geographic cluster. One relay backbone over the Bokkeveld could serve several clients, which suits a shared-infrastructure play.
- **Hex River.** The May 2026 floods (the third in five years) give a current, evidenced loss story: flood early warning, pump protection and access-road outages.

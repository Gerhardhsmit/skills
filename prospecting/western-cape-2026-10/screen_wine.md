# CTTX SCREEN: Western Cape Wine Segment (October 2026)

Prepared: 2026-10-02 · Tier: SCREEN (desk research only; no contact made, no paid enrichment used)

## Method and limits (read first)

- **Tooling limit:** WebFetch was blocked by this environment's egress proxy for every wine-industry domain tried (vanloveren.co.za, wineland.co.za, harvestsa.co.za, robertsonwinevalley.com). All evidence below therefore comes from **WebSearch result summaries** and the URLs those results returned. These are indexed snippets, not pages I read in full. Before any FACT goes into an outreach email, open the cited page and check it.
- **Tags:** FACT means stated in a search result, with its URL. INTERPRETATION means my reasoning. UNKNOWN means not found.
- Each entity was researched on its own. Where sources disagree (for example Robertson Winery hectares), both figures are shown.
- **Email patterns:** PUBLISHED means the address appears in a source. INFERRED means a guess from a pattern hint and must be checked.
- **No security or theft news specific to the target farms was found.** A regional search for 2025 theft news returned only US articles. Theft exposure is therefore marked INTERPRETATION throughout.

### Area connectivity context (applies to several candidates)
- FACT: Herotel has fibre live in Robertson and sells wireless for farms. Herotel bought BreedeNet, a Worcester WISP founded in 2005 that serves the Breede River Valley and has more than 10,000 customers. Sources: https://www.herotel.com/fibre-is-coming-to-robertson/ , https://itweb.co.za/content/VgZeyvJA391qdjX9
- FACT: IPW (ipwireless.co.za) offers wireless in Robertson, Ashton and Bonnievale, and Boland Connect offers fixed wireless in the Robertson and Bonnievale area. Sources: https://ipwireless.co.za/pages/uncapped-packages.php , https://bolandconnect.co.za/
- FACT: Wanspot's wireless footprint covers Vredendal, Klawer, Trawal, Lutzville and Vanrhynsdorp. Source: https://www.sayellow.com/south-africa/internet-service-providers-isp-wireless-in-vredendal (search summary)
- INTERPRETATION: The towns already have consumer-grade fibre and WISP options. CTTX's angle is therefore not "get internet". It is a dedicated carrier link plus an owned backbone across farms, cellars and pump sites, which the consumer WISPs do not provide as a managed private network.

## 3. Robertson Winery (Robertson). EXISTING PIPELINE RECORD

**2. Location**
- FACT: Based in Robertson town. The first cellar was a converted stone church. https://robertsonwinery.co.za/our-story/

**3. Size**
- FACT (sources conflict): 2,030 ha across the growers, or 2,400 ha. About 40 million litres sold a year. https://www.the-buyer.net/people/producer/why-robertson-is-fast-gaining-influence-as-a-top-wine-area-in-the-western-cape (search summary)

**6. Drivers:** INTERPRETATION: Grape-intake scheduling and weighbridge at harvest. A high-volume packaging plant. Grower communications.

**7. Connectivity:** UNKNOWN. In-town fibre (Herotel) is probably available.

**8. Triggers:** FACT: Pieter Siebrits was appointed CEO in November 2021. The 2025 European export push via Vinimark. https://za.linkedin.com/in/pieter-siebrits-79793744 ; https://designnews.co.za/...
